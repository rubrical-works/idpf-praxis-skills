## For-Brief

### Core Position

AB-5's prong B requires that workers perform "work outside the usual course of the hiring entity's business" — for motor carriers whose entire business is the carriage of goods, the ABC test effectively forbids the use of independent-contractor drivers, which directly affects the carrier's price/route/service. FAAAA preempts such state-law mandates per the Supreme Court's reading in *Rowe v. New Hampshire Motor Transport Ass'n*.
